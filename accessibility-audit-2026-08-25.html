The Digital Service Standard’s 13 criteria apply to public-facing Commonwealth services (informational and transactional). Inclusive design is required, not only WCAG checks.

Group: Who must comply (DDA 1992)

DDA Division 2 covers goods, services, and facilities — including websites. Banking, e-commerce, education, tourism, government, entertainment, transport, and similar public-facing services are expected to comply.

Manual

Group: Who must comply (DDA 1992)

Anyone who feels discriminated against can complain to the AHRC. Unresolved matters can go to the Federal Court. Historic precedent includes Maguire v SOCOG (1999). Overlays alone do not prevent complaints.
